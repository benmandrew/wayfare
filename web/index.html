Most lines are a road segment — a stretch of road between two junctions. Colour and thickness both show how many distinct bus and coach services use it, so trunk corridors stand out from the tail.

Trams, metros, trains and ferries have no road under them, so they are drawn from the operator's own trace and coloured by mode instead of by service count. The key below lists the ones on this map.

Hover a line for its service list. Filtering matches whole service numbers, so 4 will not match 42.

Zoomed out, the map thins the densest areas to keep tiles small, so a quiet-looking city is not necessarily a city without services. Zoom in for the complete picture.
